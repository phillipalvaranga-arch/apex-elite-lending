How it works

Straightforward steps, with a real person on the other end.

1

Send an inquiry

Tell us about your business, how much you need, and what it's for. It takes a few minutes and asks for no sensitive information.

2

Review together

We follow up to discuss your needs and securely collect the documents needed for a review, such as business bank statements.

3

Decision & terms

If approved, you get clear written terms: amount, rate, payment, and schedule, before you sign anything.

4

Get funded

Funds are sent to your business account, and you repay in fixed monthly installments.

Why Apex Elite

Built for small amounts

Many lenders won't bother with small loans. We focus on the $1,000 to $2,500 range that helps a small business cover equipment, inventory, or a cash-flow gap.

Local & personal
