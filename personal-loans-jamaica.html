Who can apply (once we launch)

Documents usually needed

Questions

Can I apply today?

Not yet. Apex Elite Lending is not yet licensed by the Bank of Jamaica, and in Jamaica it is an offence to offer microcredit without a licence. You can join the interest list, and we will contact you when we launch.

Is joining the interest list an application?

No. It only tells us you are interested. It is not an application, an approval, or a promise to lend.

What interest rate will I pay?

We have not set rates yet. Jamaican law requires a licensed lender to tell you the rate and how it is calculated in writing, and to show the Effective Annual Interest Rate (EAIR) most prominently. You will see all of this before you sign anything.

Can interest be charged on the original amount the whole time?

No. Under the Microcredit Act, interest and late penalties must be calculated on the outstanding balance, not the original amount borrowed.

Why does the form not ask for my TRN?

To protect you. We will never ask for your TRN, bank details, or card numbers through this website. After we are licensed, identity checks will be done through a secure process.

Will I be paid in Jamaican dollars?

We expect loans to be made and repaid in Jamaican dollars (JMD). The USD view in the calculator is only for comparison.

How can I check whether a lender is licensed?

The Bank of Jamaica publishes its list of licensed microcredit institutions on its website. Always check it before borrowing from anyone, including us.

Where can I complain about a lender in Jamaica?

The Consumer Affairs Commission (CAC) handles consumer complaints about licensed microcredit institutions.

Which home-buying and moving costs are planned?

None are available today. After licensing, we plan to consider personal loans for Earnest money, Home inspection, Home appraisal, Loan origination fee, Title insurance, Escrow and settlement fees, Property survey, Homeowners insurance, Prepaid property taxes, and Moving expenses. These are planned uses, not approved products, and approval is not guaranteed. If borrowed funds are used in a home purchase, the mortgage lender's approval and disclosure requirements may apply. Amounts, rates, and terms are not set.
