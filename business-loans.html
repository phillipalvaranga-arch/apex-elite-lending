Typical requirements

Examples of what we generally look for. We'll tell you exactly what's needed for your application.

  • A Texas-based business, generally operating 12+ months (6+ months may be considered with strong owner credit)
  • An EIN or assumed-name (DBA) certificate
  • 3 to 6 months of business bank statements, with deposits that comfortably support the payment
  • Valid government ID for each owner, plus identity verification
  • A credit review, done only with your signed consent
  • A personal guaranty from owners of 20% or more, and a signed business-purpose certification
  • A clear, legitimate business use for the funds

We do not lend to illegal businesses, for gambling, or to refinance other lenders or merchant cash advances. Approval is not guaranteed.
