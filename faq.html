Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply?

Texas small businesses and business owners who need funds for a business purpose, such as equipment, inventory, or cash flow.

How much can I borrow?

Business-purpose loans currently range from $1,000 to $2,500. First-time borrowers may be offered a smaller amount.

What is the interest rate?

Rates are fixed and never exceed 18% APR. Your rate depends on our review and appears in your written agreement before you sign.

Do you offer personal loans?

Not yet. Personal loans for borrowers in Jamaica are coming soon, launching once licensed by the Bank of Jamaica. Personal loans in Texas still need Texas licensing. Today we make business-purpose loans only and are not a consumer lender. See Personal Loans: Jamaica.

Can I use a business loan for personal expenses?

No. Loans must be used primarily for business purposes, and you'll sign a business-purpose certification.

Do you check credit?

We may review credit as part of underwriting, only with your signed consent. A credit history alone doesn't decide an application.

Is approval guaranteed?

No. Every application is reviewed against the same written criteria, and approval is never guaranteed.

Why doesn't the online form ask for my SSN or bank details?

To protect you. The website form only starts the conversation. If we move forward, we'll collect any sensitive documents through a secure method, never by regular email or website form.

How do I repay?

Through fixed monthly payments on a set schedule. Automatic payments may be available with your written authorization, which you can revoke.

Are there prepayment penalties?

Prepayment terms will be stated in your loan agreement. Ask us before you apply.
