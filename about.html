About Apex Elite Lending

A Frisco, Texas company focused on small business funding.

Apex Elite Lending LLC is a new, independently owned lending company based in Frisco, Texas. We started with a simple idea: small businesses sometimes need a small amount of capital, and they deserve a lender who treats a $1,500 request as seriously as a large one.

We're starting deliberately small, making business-purpose loans of $1,000 to $2,500 at no more than 18% APR, so we can serve each borrower personally and build a track record the right way.

Our commitments

  • Transparency: every cost and payment in writing before you sign.
  • Fairness: the same written criteria for every applicant, in line with fair-lending laws.
  • Respect: professional, lawful communication at every stage, including collections.
  • Privacy: we collect only what we need and protect it carefully.
  • Honesty: no "guaranteed approval," no hidden fees, no pressure.

What's next

As the company grows, we intend to apply for Texas licensing to offer personal installment loans. Until then, we offer business-purpose loans only.
